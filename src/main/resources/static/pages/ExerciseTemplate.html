Barbell Bench Press

fitness_center Chest
speed Intermediate
build Barbell

info Description

A foundational upper-body compound exercise targeting the pectoralis major, anterior deltoids, and triceps brachii. Performed lying supine on a flat bench, pressing a barbell upward from sternum height until full arm extension.

format_list_numbered Instructions

  1. Lie flat on the bench with your eyes directly under the racked barbell.
  2. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width with wrists straight.
  3. Unrack and lower the bar under control toward mid-chest while tucking elbows at roughly 45 degrees.
  4. Press the bar upward explosively back to the starting position without locking elbows violently.
